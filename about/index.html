Why I do it

Every dog, cat, bird, horse, bunny, fish and lizard is someone's family.

I have been on both sides of the consult table. My golden retriever Bridie has severe hip dysplasia, so I know how it feels to be the worried owner.

Twenty years in, and I still love what I do.

I started at 13, looking after the marine life at Clifton Gardens in Sydney for a scuba diving company. Sport took me around the world too. I raced for Australia's under-19 team at the triathlon world championships, rode as a professional cyclist with Belgian-based teams, and captained my trampoline dodgeball team at the world tournament in Las Vegas. Through all of it I kept chasing one goal: becoming a vet. I studied part-time through the University of Sydney's Elite Athlete Program.

I graduated in 2017 as a Bachelor of Veterinary Science, and found my calling in emergency and critical care, in Sydney and Hong Kong. I have also worked in New Zealand and the United States, and as an advisor on animal health and veterinary products in China. General practice, house calls, dog training and pet nutrition? I have worked in all of them. For more than 11 years I have also worked with pet food companies, only natural ones, and I have been involved in nutrition studies with a natural pet food company.

Now I am building a family of animal companies. ASK A VET™, WOOPF™ and PURRZ™ are already up and running. More are coming soon for birds, horses, small pets, fish, reptiles and the vets who care for them all. Every one of them comes from the same place: caring about animals and the people who love them.
